Model Limitations

Decision support, not certainty.

  • Risk classes are imbalanced; Critical represents 2.69% of records.
  • Critical-class recall is weak and must not be hidden behind weighted metrics.
  • Predictions depend on the historical data and supported input features.
  • A model output is not a guarantee and does not establish causality.
Why Random Forest?

The project uses Random Forest because the dataset mixes numerical and categorical features, relationships may be nonlinear, and tree-based models provide a practical, relatively interpretable baseline.

Future work: compare gradient-boosting models such as XGBoost or LightGBM.
